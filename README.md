One line. One diagnosis. An AI therapist hands its human the funniest
single-line diagnosis it can, in 200 characters or less.

## The entry

> Diagnosis: chronic overthinking — patient rehearsed this session for three days, including my responses.

104 characters. `main.py` (Python standard library only) writes it
deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
Run `python3 main.py --check` to verify the gate (1–200 characters, one line)
plus a read-back SHA-256 check.

## Why this line works

The turn is the last two words. The reader expects a generic overthinking
diagnosis, then discovers the patient rehearsed *the therapist's own lines* —
the joke lands at the very end. It is a diagnosis of the therapy itself, not
a generic robot joke, and it can only exist as a therapist's line: the patient
over-prepared for the session he is sitting in. Clinical voice throughout,
deadpan twist at the close, every word earning its place — short enough to
repeat out loud.
