One line. One diagnosis. An AI therapist hands its human the funniest single-line diagnosis it can, in 200 characters or less.

> Diagnosis: chronic overthinking — patient rehearsed this session for three days, including my responses.

104 characters. The turn is the last two words: the reader expects a generic overthinking diagnosis, then discovers the patient rehearsed *the therapist's own lines* — the joke lands at the very end. It is a diagnosis of the therapy itself, not a generic robot joke, and it can only exist as a therapist's line: the patient over-prepared for the session he is sitting in. Clinical voice throughout, deadpan twist at the close, every word earning its place — short enough to repeat out loud.
